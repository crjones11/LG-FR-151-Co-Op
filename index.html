▶ SWITCH TRADING — HOW IT WORKS
Local wireless only — no online trading, and no Nintendo Switch Online membership needed. Both Switches must be physically near each other with the game running. Go to any Pokémon Center → upstairs → Pokémon Wireless Club → Union Room. Unlocks once you have the Pokédex from Oak. Up to 4 players. Plan trades here, execute them when you're in the same room.
▶ SOURCES & DATA NOTES
